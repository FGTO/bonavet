# Household-scoped data from day one

BoNavet starts as one family's wall dashboard but is meant to become a paid service for other households. Every piece of data is therefore owned by a Household and every query is scoped to one, even while only a single Household exists. Sign-up, billing and household self-service are out of v1; retrofitting tenancy later would touch every table and query, so we pay the small cost now.
